How search results differed / 검색 결과의 차이

The government source is the primary reference for official franchise disclosures. Edaily provides a news summary that can help interpret the release. Semosun presents brand-level counts in a searchable ranking format and explains that its location metric excludes directly operated stores. Search snippets and secondary rankings are useful cross-checks, but the official source should take priority when figures differ.
